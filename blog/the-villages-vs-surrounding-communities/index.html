The Villages vs. Surrounding Communities: What Buyers Should Compare

Compare The Villages, Oxford, Wildwood, Lady Lake, Summerfield, Belleview, and Ocala by jurisdiction, housing, services, and property-specific costs.

By Patrick Hammon · Coldwell Banker Ellison Realty · Published November 24, 2026

There is no single “best” answer to whether you should search The Villages, Oxford, Wildwood, Lady Lake, Summerfield, Belleview, or Ocala. These names do not describe the same kind of place: one is a large planned community crossing county lines, some are incorporated cities or a town, and others are community or mailing-area names.

That matters because boundaries can affect who provides services, which tax authorities appear on the bill, and which district, association, or amenity arrangements apply. Compare actual addresses and current documents instead of treating a ZIP code or marketing label as a property description.

A practical comparison

The table is a starting point for questions, not a claim that every property in a place has the same housing type, service, or fee. Current inventory changes, and a nearby address can be under a different jurisdiction.

City limit, city/county tax lines, actual utility provider and availability, district maps, HOA documents, and routes permitted for the transportation mode you plan to use.

Compare homes with a Belleview city address against nearby Marion County properties; municipal and county services may differ. City boundary, utility service, city/county tax authorities, zoning, HOA or district obligations, and current property documents.

Compare a specific Ocala city property with nearby Marion County addresses; current inventory and property types vary across neighborhoods. How to compare the actual homes

For travel, test the routes at the times you expect to use them. If golf-cart access is important, verify the legal route and any community access rules; geographic proximity alone does not establish access.

Why city limits and mailing addresses matter

Sumter County lists Wildwood as an incorporated city and provides county GIS tools. Wildwood also publishes a city boundary map and utility-service information. In the Town of Lady Lake, planning and zoning is administered by the Town. Belleview and Ocala likewise have municipal governments. By contrast, a community or postal name such as Oxford or Summerfield is not itself proof that a parcel is within a municipal boundary.

The Villages is a multi-county planned community with separate Community Development Districts. A home may be described using a neighborhood name while also having a county, city, district, association, and amenity arrangement that must be checked independently. The district maps and the county property appraiser's parcel record are better starting points than a generalized map pin.

For ownership costs, separate:

  • County and municipal property taxes.
  • CDD taxes or non-ad valorem assessments shown on the tax notice.
  • HOA assessments and governing documents.
  • Any separate amenity or recreation access fee.
  • Insurance, utilities, maintenance, and other property-level costs.

Do not treat these as one “community fee,” and do not compare a countywide listing statistic with a city-specific figure as if they were the same measure.

Choose based on your own routine, not a ranking

Visit each finalist, check the routes and services you will actually use, and read the documents that govern the property.
